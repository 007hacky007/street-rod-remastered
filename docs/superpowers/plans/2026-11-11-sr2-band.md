# Street Rod 2 band music Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** With `--music remastered`, Street Rod 2's music is played by Street Rod 1's small band, following the game's own sequencers, for both AdLib and PC speaker.

**Architecture:** The band's synthesiser and score code (`synth.c`, `arrange.c`) move from `src/sound/` to `common/band/` and are compiled into each game's core. In the AdLib path, `src2/sound/mus.c` keeps driving the OPL chip exactly as now and also hands every note event to `src2/sound/band.c`, which maps MSR.SND timbres to the band's instruments and the percussion voices to its drums. The host mutes the OPL voices marked as music while a music synth is set, so only the band is heard and the effects stay on the chip. In the PC speaker path, the speaker sequencer hands its notes to `band.c`, which plays them through Street Rod 1's arrangement of the speaker tune (`song_parse`, `arrange`), as SR1's `music_rm.c` does.

**Tech Stack:** C11, SDL3, Nuked-OPL3 (host), CMake/ctest, POSIX sh + Python 3 for the audio checks; the launcher in C++17/wxWidgets.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-street-rod-2-design.md`, section "Remastered music" (and the launcher's Music choice). Plan 3 of 5; plans 1 (engine) and 2 (launcher) are done.

## Global Constraints

- The band takes its notes from the game's sequencers: song choice, tempo, loops and pauses stay the game's, including the 136 s silence after the first title song.
- Sound effects stay as they are (AdLib or speaker), at the effects volume. The band plays at the music volume.
- `--music original|remastered`: default original in the game, Remastered in the launcher, as SR1.
- With the PC speaker, Remastered plays the speaker tune (`DS_spk_bg_song`, DS:5594, 14 sections) through Street Rod 1's arrangement. The game clock is never changed.
- Nothing derived from the songs is committed or shipped: the band reads the songs from the game files at run time.
- Street Rod 1 does not change: `tools/sr1-codecheck.sh f44688e HEAD --allow host.c.o --allow synth.c.o` reports no other differing object, and `host_sr1_audio_unchanged` and `music` pass.
- Faithful-code changes in `src2/` carry an `ENH:` marker.
- No macOS username, no real name or email, no AI attribution, no Unicode dashes in anything committed. Commit messages: one short imperative line.

## Review Focus

1. With Remastered and AdLib, the police siren and the engine must still sound on the chip at the effects volume, and after the siren the music on that voice must go back to the band, not to the chip. (Task 2, `opl_music_muted_by_synth`; Task 3, `sr2_band_siren`.)
2. Music off (M), a race start (`music_stop`) or sound off (Ctrl) must silence the band at once, with no note left ringing. (Task 3, `sr2_band_stop_silences`.)
3. A note-on on a voice that is already sounding must replace that note, as on the chip (each AdLib voice plays one note), not stack a second one. (Task 3, `sr2_band_retrigger`.)
4. With Original music nothing may change: the OPL output and the speaker output are the same as before. (Task 3 and Task 4, the existing `sr2_music`, `sr2_volumes` and `sr2_smoke` stay green; `band_on` false makes every hook return at once.)
5. Remastered with the PC speaker must not leave the speaker's tune audible under the band. (Task 4, the `speaker_remastered_music_0` case is quiet.)

### Task 3: The band follows Street Rod 2's AdLib songs

**Files:**
- Create: `src2/sound/band.h`, `src2/sound/band.c`
- Modify: `common/band/synth.c`, `common/band/synth.h` (a `DRUM_TOM`), `src2/sound/mus.c` (hooks, `ENH:`), `src2/sound/slots.c` (`band_tick` after the sound part of the timer tick, `ENH:`)
- Test: `tests/test_sr2_band.c` (new ctest `sr2_band`, under `SRE_TEST_GAME2_DIR` like `sr2_music`)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (`band.h`):
  - `typedef struct { int kind; int inst; int drum; } BandPart;` with `kind` one of `BAND_NONE`, `BAND_NOTE`, `BAND_DRUM`.
  - `BandPart band_part_of(const char *timbre);` (pure). The map:
    - `bass1`, `bass2`: INST_BASS.
    - `jazzguit`, `guitar1`, `elguit1`, `fuzguit2`: INST_GUITAR.
    - `piano1`, `elpiano1`: INST_EPIANO.
    - `moogsynt`, `syn3`: INST_VIBES.
    - `bdrum1`: DRUM_KICK. `tom1`, `logdrum1`, `logdrum2`: DRUM_TOM.
    - `snare1`, `crash`, `cymbal1`: DRUM_SNARE. `hihat1`, `hihat2`: DRUM_BRUSH.
    - Any other name (the effects: `police`, `motor`, `turn1` ...): BAND_NONE.
  - `BandPart band_perc_part(int voice);` for the AdLib percussion voices: 6 KICK, 7 SNARE, 8 TOM, 9 SNARE, 10 BRUSH.
  - `bool band_enable(bool with_host);`, `void band_disable(void);`, `extern bool band_on;` As SR1's `music_rm_enable`: `with_host` sets `synth_init(host_audio_rate())` and `host_set_music_synth`.
  - From `mus.c` (all return at once unless `band_on`):
    - `void band_adlib_program(int v, int index);` (the bank's timbre name by index, via `snd_timbre_lookup`'s table).
    - `void band_adlib_note_on(int v, int note, int vel);` releases the voice's sounding note first; `note` is a MIDI pitch (note 60 is middle C); `vel` 1..127 becomes 0..1.
    - `void band_adlib_note_off(int v);`
    - `void band_adlib_bend(int v, u16 bend);` 0x2000 is the centre, the song header's bend range in semitones.
    - `void band_adlib_perc(bool on);`
    - `void band_adlib_all_off(void);` from `mus_stop`, `mus_play` and `mus_resume`.
  - `void band_tick(void);` every timer tick: all quiet when `DS_sound_on` is 0 or nothing plays.
  - `int band_notes_started(void);` for the tests.
- `synth.h`: `DRUM_TOM` after `DRUM_BRUSH`; `synth_drum_pitched(int kind, float midi, float vel)` for the tom. SR1 never calls either. That is why `synth.c.o` is on the codecheck allow list.
- Builder's call: aftertouch (`0xA0`, three to seven per song) is not passed to the band. Pans as SR1's `pan_of`, the drums centred.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**, in the style of `test_sr2_music.c` (headless, `host_set_realtime(false)`, `band_enable(false)`, rendering `synth_render` itself):
  - `sr2_band_parts`: every name of the map above, `police` and `motor` give BAND_NONE, and `band_perc_part` for 6..10.
  - `sr2_band_plays_title`: `snd_bank_and_intro_load`, `adlib_music_init`, `mus_play(1)`, 3 s. Then `band_notes_started() > 20` and the RMS of 0.5 s of `synth_render` > 0.01.
  - `sr2_band_stop_silences`: after that, `mus_stop()` then 1 s. The RMS of the next 0.25 s < 0.001.
  - `sr2_band_retrigger`: two `band_adlib_note_on(0, 60, 95)` in a row, then `band_adlib_note_off(0)` and 1 s. The RMS < 0.001; a stacked note would still ring.
  - `sr2_band_siren`: as `sr2_music` (siren on voice 2, `mus_resume`), and `band_notes_started()` grows after the resume.

  Run `cmake --build build-mac && ctest --test-dir build-mac -R sr2_band --output-on-failure`. Expected: build FAIL (`band.h` missing).

- [ ] **Step 2: Implement** `band.c`, the drum, and the hooks in `mus.c` next to each `adlib_*` call of the 0x80, 0x90, 0xC0 and 0xE0 events, `mus_stop`, `mus_play`, `mus_resume`, and `mus_set_bank` / `mus_play` for the percussion mode. Call `host_audio_sync()` before each synth change when `with_host`, as `music_rm.c` does.
